About EverySolver

EverySolver is a growing set of free calculators that solve real work problems in plain numbers. The first section covers manufacturing and cost, for the people who make and buy physical products: plant and production managers, process and industrial engineers, cost accountants, buyers, importers and owners of small factories and job shops.

Who builds it

The calculators are written by an engineer who spent twelve years developing production equipment for display manufacturing and then moved into cost management. Much of that work was the same handful of questions asked again for every new line, machine or supplier: what does this part really cost once it lands, what does an hour of this machine cost, how much is scrap costing us, and will this investment pay back. Each calculator here started as a spreadsheet built to answer one of those questions.

How we build each calculator

  • One question per page. Every calculator answers a single, specific cost question rather than trying to be a general tool.
  • Formulas are always shown. You can check every result by hand or rebuild it in a spreadsheet; nothing is hidden.
  • Worked examples are checked. The example numbers in each guide are recalculated against the live calculator before a page is published.
  • Practical caveats. Each guide lists the mistakes that most often make the result wrong in real plants, such as using the wrong yield or the wrong duty basis.

Your data stays with you

All calculations run in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server or stored, except your currency choice, which your browser remembers for the next visit. Share links contain your inputs in the address so that a colleague sees the same numbers; they are not stored anywhere else.

Limits

The results are estimates for planning and decision support. Duty rates, tax rules and accounting conventions vary by country and company, so confirm important figures with your customs broker, accountant or finance team before you commit money.
