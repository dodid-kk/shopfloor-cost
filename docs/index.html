Manufacturing cost calculators

Quick, transparent answers to the cost questions that come up on the shop floor and in purchasing: what an imported part really costs, what a machine hour costs, how efficient a line is, how much scrap costs a year, what to charge and whether a machine pays for itself. Every calculator shows its formula, runs in your browser and gives you a link you can share with the numbers filled in.
